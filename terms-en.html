Guardy is provided by 保險好鄰居 (“we”). It’s a tool that helps you handle a car accident scene: location, on-scene steps, a photo checklist, insurers’ claim hotlines, and general guidance on mediation and compensation.

By using Guardy, you agree to these terms.

2. What Guardy is not

Not an emergency service. If you need the police or an ambulance, call 110 or 119 directly. Guardy only helps you make that call faster; it doesn’t report anything to anyone on your behalf.

Not legal advice. Information in the app about fault, mediation and compensation is general and doesn’t replace a lawyer.

Not a promise of insurance payment. What’s covered and how much is decided by your policy and your insurer. The app’s calculations only organize items you have documents for.

Not an insurance company. Guardy doesn’t represent, and isn’t commissioned by, any insurer. The claim hotlines in the app are publicly available numbers, listed for your convenience.
